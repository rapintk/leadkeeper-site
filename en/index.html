Every lead answered fast, followed up to the end, and visible to the owner every week.

LeadKeeper is a Thailand-based implementation partner. We set up lead response and sales pipeline systems for Thai SMEs on LINE and CRM platforms the client owns — then maintain them every month. We don't sell software; we make it work.

The problems we solve

  • Businesses pay for ads, but leads get slow replies or no follow-up.Leads who message at night or on weekends often go to the competitor who answers first.
  • Customer conversations live in salespeople's personal LINE accounts.Owners can't see the pipeline, quotations go unfollowed, and customers leave when a salesperson leaves.

Services

1. Lead Response System

For businesses running ads for high-value leads

  • Reply to leads within minutes with qualifying questions
  • Book site visits or calls
  • 30-day follow-up sequences
  • Lead reports compared against ad spend

2. Sales Pipeline System

For B2B companies with a sales team

  • CRM and pipeline the owner can see every day
  • Company LINE Official Account instead of salespeople's personal LINE
  • Automated quotation follow-up rules
  • Owner dashboard, team training and monthly reviews

3. Lead Response Lite

For small teams using one LINE Official Account

  • FAQ bot
  • Lead qualification form and appointment booking
  • Automated follow-up messages
